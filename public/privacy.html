Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This website shows Zurich's public transport on a 3D map. It collects as little personal data as possible: no user accounts, no cookies, no third-party tracking or analytics, no advertising. Visits are counted anonymously by this website’s own server (section 3).

2. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Hostinger International Ltd., Larnaca, Cyprus, on servers in Germany. When you open the website, the hosting provider's web server records technical information about the request: IP address, date and time, requested address (URL) and status code, referrer (the page you came from, if your browser sends it) and browser type (user agent).

This is needed to deliver the website and to detect and prevent misuse and attacks. The website's own software keeps no access logs; the hosting provider deletes its logs automatically after a limited period. This data is not combined with other sources or used to identify visitors.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 31 Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, FADP; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR where applicable). The Swiss Federal Council recognises the countries of the EU/EEA as providing an adequate level of data protection.

3. Anonymous visitor statistics

To know how many people use the website and which features are useful, this website’s own server counts visits. No external analytics service is used, and nothing is passed on. When a page loads, your browser sends: the page, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer, from the screen size), your browser language, the website you came from (if your browser shares it), a campaign tag if the link contained one (e.g. ?src=insta) and whether you had visited on an earlier day. It also reports which features you use (for example clicking a vehicle or searching; each at most once per visit) and how long the visit lasted.

To count each visitor only once per day, the server combines your IP address and browser type with a random key and keeps only the resulting code (a one-way hash). The key changes every day and is deleted at midnight together with the codes, so visitors can’t be recognised from one day to the next or identified. Your IP address is not stored. Only daily totals are kept. Whether you visited before is decided by your browser, which stores the date of your last visit in its local storage (see section 4).

Nothing is counted when your browser has “Global Privacy Control” or “Do Not Track” switched on. Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding and improving the website (Art. 31 FADP; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR where applicable).

4. Settings stored in your browser

Your display settings (for example which layers are switched on, map style, graphics quality) are saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device only. They are never sent to the server. The date of your last visit is stored there too, for the statistics in section 3; only “visited before: yes/no” is sent. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Fonts and program libraries are served from this website's own server. To display the map, your browser loads data directly from the following services, which therefore receive your IP address and browser information:

6. Your rights

You can request information about the personal data held about you, and have it corrected or deleted, or object to its processing, by writing to contact@zuerich3d.ch. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country of residence.
