Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This website shows Zurich's public transport on a 3D map. It collects as little personal data as possible: no user accounts, no cookies, no tracking or analytics, no advertising.

1. Who is responsible

Atahan Caramanolis
Email: contact@zuerich3d.ch

2. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Hostinger International Ltd., Larnaca, Cyprus, on servers in Germany. When you open the website, the hosting provider's web server records technical information about the request: IP address, date and time, requested address (URL) and status code, referrer (the page you came from, if your browser sends it) and browser type (user agent).

This is needed to deliver the website and to detect and prevent misuse and attacks. The website's own software keeps no access logs; the hosting provider deletes its logs automatically after a limited period. This data is not combined with other sources or used to identify visitors.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 31 Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, FADP; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR where applicable). The Swiss Federal Council recognises the countries of the EU/EEA as providing an adequate level of data protection.

3. Settings stored in your browser

Your display settings (for example which layers are switched on, map style, graphics quality) are saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device only. They are never sent to the server. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

4. External services your browser contacts

Fonts and program libraries are served from this website's own server. To display the map, your browser loads data directly from the following services, which therefore receive your IP address and browser information:

ServicePurposeWhen
OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org)Base map, map labels and building outlinesAlways
swisstopo, Federal Office of Topography, Switzerland (wmts.geo.admin.ch)Aerial imageryOnly when you select the “Satellite” basemap

Timetable, real-time and weather data (from opentransportdata.swiss and Open-Meteo.com) are downloaded by the server itself; no information about you is passed on to these services.

5. Your rights

You can request information about the personal data held about you, and have it corrected or deleted, or object to its processing, by writing to contact@zuerich3d.ch. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country of residence.
